I am a founding Programme Director at [ARIA](https://www.aria.org.uk/) and Honorary Associate Professor at [UCL](https://www.ucl.ac.uk). I am an applied physicist by training, having spent ten years developing photonic technologies to accelerate quantum and classical computing, initially at [MIT](https://www.rle.mit.edu/qp/) and then at the [Niels Bohr Institute](https://nbi.ku.dk/english/research/quantum-optics-and-photonics/).

I later moved into systems neuroscience, where I developed optical technologies for high-speed, large-scale interrogation of neural circuits in vivo at UCL.

At ARIA I lead the [Precision Neurotechnologies programme](https://www.aria.org.uk/precision-neurotechnologies/), which is developing next-generation circuit-level tools to help understand and repair the human brain. I completed my PhD at the University of Bristol in 2015.
