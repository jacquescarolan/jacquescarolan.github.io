I’m a scientist and programme builder working on ambitious neurotechnology. At ARIA, I lead the [Precision Neurotechnologies programme](https://www.aria.org.uk/precision-neurotechnologies/), which is developing new circuit-level tools to help understand and repair the human brain.

My work draws on applied physics, photonics, engineering and systems neuroscience. This site brings together current work, writing and selected conversations.
